Translate It! - Translation for Text, Pages, Documents, and Live Dubbing

Translate whatever you need, right in your browser.

Translate It can handle selected text, input fields, page elements, full pages, PDFs, subtitles, and text inside images. It also includes Live Dubbing, which lets you hear audio playing in a browser tab dubbed into your chosen language in real time.

You can choose the translation service that works best for you, including traditional providers, AI services, and local or offline options where available.

---

✓ What You Can Do

- Instant Selection: Select text on any page and view the translation right next to it.
- Translate While Typing: Translate text directly inside input fields before sending it.
- Element Translation: Click a page element to translate it in place while keeping the layout intact.
- Whole Page Translation: Translate complete pages, including dynamically loaded content and infinite-scroll pages.
- Mouse Hover Translation: Hold a modifier key and hover over text to translate a word, sentence, or content block.
- Screen Capture & OCR: Select an area containing text in an image, video, or other non-selectable content and translate it.
- PDF Translation: Open local or online PDFs, translate pages, use bilingual views, run OCR on scanned documents, and export translated content to TXT, Markdown, or HTML.
- Live Dubbing: Hear audio from your browser tab dubbed into your chosen language in real time. Use it with YouTube, Netflix, Google Meet, online courses, and other websites that play audio.
- Subtitle Translation: Translate SRT subtitle files while preserving timestamps and formatting.
- Bilingual Subtitles: Follow along with subtitles for both the original speech and the dubbed translation.
- Smart Dictionary: View definitions, synonyms, examples, and pronunciation information.
- Text to Speech: Listen to translated text using supported voices.
- History & Export: Keep and export previous translations when needed.

---

✓ Providers and Flexibility

Translate It supports more than 10 translation providers, including Google, Microsoft, DeepL, OpenAI-compatible, DeepSeek and more.

You are not tied to a single provider. Choose the service that fits your needs, available API keys, cost preferences, or privacy requirements.

For supported AI providers, optimization controls let you choose between larger, more efficient requests and faster parallel processing.

---


✓ Built for Everyday Browsing

Translate It is designed for regular browsing as well as long pages, dynamic websites, and continuously loaded content.

Features such as progressive translation, lazy loading, and configurable processing help keep translation responsive without forcing every task through the same workflow.

---

✓ Supported Languages

Language support depends on the selected provider.

Major providers support a wide range of languages, including English, Persian, Japanese, French, German, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Korean, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, and many more.
